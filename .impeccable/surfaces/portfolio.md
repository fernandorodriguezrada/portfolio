## Job and Audience
- **Visitor:** Engineering leaders, technical recruiters, developers, and collaborators.
- **Context:** Looking for an authentic, hands-on software and systems engineer who builds robust, production-grade applications with clean architecture and database rigor.
- **Mode:** Experience — the technical artifacts and systems thinking lead; the UI is a sober, classic studio monograph with tactile craft.

- **Primary Goal:** Provide instant, effortless readability of Fernando's projects, architecture breakdowns, skills, and direct contact options in both English and Spanish.
- **Profile:** Fernando Rodríguez Rada — Computer Science Student at IUTECP (Instituto Universitario de Tecnología Elías Calixto Pompa), Guatire, Miranda, Venezuela.
- **Flagship Proof ("The Big 3"):**
  1. **Cambi:** PWA, PHP 8.4 BCV multi-tier scraper with cron caching, zero-server-PII local-first Pago Móvil manager, offline QR generation, dynamic arithmetic expression parser.
  2. **QR_IUTECP:** Institutional attendance management system under PDS Skeleton, PHP 8.2+ PDO & MariaDB 11.8, CLI migration engine, 32-character ephemeral QR sessions with 5-minute TTL, browser-based video stream decoding via `jsQR`, 4-stage scan validation pipeline, 3-tier RBAC with audit logging.
  3. **Together:** Fastify 5 WebSocket real-time audio sync platform (tested Venezuela <-> Lithuania), host-authoritative playback anchors with RTT/clock-offset estimation and 5s drift correction, 6-character room codes, PostgreSQL (Neon serverless + Docker local), React 18.
- **Secondary Projects Directory:** Playful (Rust/Kitty), Terra (PHP/MariaDB), Colmena (PHP/TCPDF), Mercader FBA (Node.js/React), Python Monitor (Flask).

## Selected Direction
- **Visual Identity:** Sober classic studio portfolio inspired by architectural monographs and craft web design.
- **Themes:**
  - **Light Mode (Default):** Warm linen background (`#FAF8F5`), pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`), soft sandstone borders (`#E2DBD2`), deep charcoal headers (`#1C1917`), dark gray body (`#443E38`), sober light brown / saddle leather accent (`#8C532B`).
  - **Dark Mode:** Warm matte dark roast background (`#171514`), elevated slate cards (`#211D1B`), borders (`#38322D`), off-white headers (`#F7F3EE`), muted gray body (`#C9C1B6`), sandstone beige accent (`#DEB085`).
- **Typography:** `Epilogue` (bold uppercase section headers) + `Source Sans 3` (clean editorial body prose) + `JetBrains Mono` (technical tags/code).
- **Navigation & Controls:** Solid matte dark top bar (`#141211`) with circular `FR` monogram badge, clean uppercase navigation links, instant `EN | ES` language switcher, and Sun/Moon theme toggle button.
- **About Me Section:** 2-column split with Polaroid-style tilted photo frame on the left and first-person bio on the right, followed by "Things I Enjoy" and "What I Can Do" sub-row with warm accent indicators.
- **Section Headers:** Centered with solid horizontal rules on both sides (`─── ABOUT ME ───` / `─── SOBRE MÍ ───`) and warm accent highlight on the second word.
- **Bilingual Engine:** Zero-reload `en`/`es` translation dictionary in `app.js` with `localStorage` persistence.

## Scope and Boundaries
- **Fidelity:** Production-ready static site deployable on GitHub Pages.
- **Delivery Target:** `index.html`, `style.css`, and `app.js`.
- **Anti-goals:** Zero terminal or CLI costumes, zero neon glow, zero glassmorphism, zero fake progress bars, zero corporate buzzwords.
